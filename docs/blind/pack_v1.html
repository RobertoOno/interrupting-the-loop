MCMC is a class of algorithms for sampling from probability distributions based on constructing a Markov Chain that has the desired distribution as its stationary distribution. The state of the chain after a large number of steps is then used as a sample from the desired distribution.
In other words, MCMC algorithms allow us to use Monte Carlo methods to approximate quantities that we cannot compute directly because they involve high-dimensional integrals or complicated probability distributions.
The key idea in MCMC is that we don’t actually need our Markov Chain to converge to our desired stationary distribution in order to use it as a way of generating samples from that distribution. All we need is for our chain’s stationary distribution to be close
