The PDFs in this repository are the delivered versions identified in the root README. This update adds the sources of papers 5 and 7 and the published analysis code missing from the earlier mirror. It preserves the delivered manuscript text. Raw evidence remains in its versioned deposits.

## Qualifications from the 18–19 October retests

These follow-up observations postdate some delivered manuscripts. They qualify the scope of earlier interpretations; they do not rewrite frozen protocols or archived scores.

- **P3, delivered bridge versus no bridge:** all 27 sessions of the output-ceiling retest solved, across its conditions. The shared transfer baseline without a supplied bridge solved 8/8, compared with 0/4 at the original smaller budget, using roughly 9–11 times the tokens of the bridge condition. In these development worlds, the supplied bridge saved reasoning budget; the earlier contrast does not establish an expansion of capacity. The paper 5 proposer sensitivity had already shown the target-only proposer producing bridges in 3/4 at 65,536 tokens.
- **Program 4, tomography gate:** a new generation-zero retest with more proposals did not pass the registered gate: code contrast +0.083, p = 0.068; words +0.333, p = 0.0002. The rule required both. This qualifies the tomography opportunity; the earlier form-versus-description result is a separate contrast.
- **Resource-limited negatives:** sessions stopped by an imposed token, call or time limit are censored observations. They must be counted separately from completed failures. Earlier negative claims must retain their measured budgets; the studies do not establish a general inability of a model to produce an operator.

The follow-up read documents, numerical summaries and checksum manifests are included under `docs/post_publication/`. They are explicitly later evidence, outside the pre-registration of the delivered papers. Follow-up model responses remain outside this Git tree.
