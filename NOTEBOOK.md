# Public research record

This index links the dated protocols and evidence supporting the delivered papers. The full working notebook also contains personal and unpublished material; its historical public excerpt in `docs/PLANO.md` is frozen. This page replaces the former promise of a full English notebook translation.

- Papers 1–4: the [original run-data deposits](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22714138) and the [October correction evidence](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23163299). The latter includes `preregistrations.tar.gz`, with dated excerpts and source references, and an index inside each paper archive.
- Paper 5: [programs 5–7](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22713807), including their pre-registrations, single reads and raw traces. See the transfer, Lean-bridge and factorial archives respectively.
- Paper 6: the [October evidence deposit](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23163299), `paper6_verified_lean_proofs.tar.gz`, with the A2–A9 protocols, training data, verifier reports and analysis artifacts.
- Paper 7: the [report deposit](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23562924), with the R1 pre-registration and amendments, frozen documents, reference set, replay, R2 pilots, redaction log and report review rounds.

The studies' original pre-registration commits belong to the private research history. Their identifiers are preserved in the deposits as provenance references; they need not resolve in this public repository, whose older history was filtered. Public release commits do not claim to be new pre-registrations.

See [REPRODUCING.md](REPRODUCING.md) for restoration instructions and [publication notes](docs/PUBLICATION_NOTES.md) for qualifications added after the delivered versions.
