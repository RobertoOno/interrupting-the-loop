\begin{abstract}
A research program on machine novelty has intervened at three depths of
a language model --- the token distribution, the context, and the
weights --- and certified the limit of each. This paper opens the fourth:
the residual stream during live generation. We introduce a rank-1/2
operator algebra on hidden states (translations toward model-derived
concept directions, closed-form rotors, projections, repulsion from a
moving mean; an anchor and a norm-shell as geometric guards; doses in
units of the premise-state norm, which transfer across models), and
evaluate it under a deception-resistant protocol: judged surprise,
connection and coherence on generated-only windows, plus an exact
program verifier. Five pre-registered findings. (i)~\emph{The temporal
law}: with direction, layer and per-token dose fixed, continuous
injection lowers judged surprise (dose--response, $p=0.81$ against, coherence
collapsing), while the same directions pulsed for 32 tokens every 300
raise it ($+0.90$, $p=0.006$). (ii)~Geometric guards do not repair
damage; they license dose --- and at guarded dose 2.0 the pulse reaches
statistical \emph{parity} with the best prompt-side intervention we
know, text interruption ($3.40$ vs $3.64$, two-sided $p=0.53$;
connection identical), without spending a token of the stream.
(iii)~The result replicates in-family at 14B with an exact tie ($3.48$
vs $3.48$; replication $p=0.002$). (iv)~Mechanistically, habituated
generation carries a \emph{silent schematic orbit} --- matched
token--state periodicity without verbatim repetition --- and both
winning interventions flatten the internal spectrum. (v)~On verified
code search the same operators buy no tail depth at all: best distances
to classical baselines are identical across arms, dissociating judged
experience from discovered value. We also map the boundaries: late
layers act as token bias, early layers destabilize into register
basins, aligned models break under forced continuation, and repulsion
from a moving reference is self-limiting. We release the instrument,
every cell, and the dated pre-registrations.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Can a language model produce something genuinely new, and which
interventions actually help? A sequence of studies has approached this
question one substrate at a time, under a fixed measurement discipline:
judged surprise, connection and coherence on windows containing only
generated text (so that the judge can never be shown the intervention
itself)~\citep{ono2026interrupting}, and, in verifiable domains, an
exact evaluator with classical baselines~\citep{ono2026mass}. At the
\emph{token} level, anti-probable sampling bought only surface novelty.
At the \emph{context} level, periodically interrupting a habituated
stream with a new subject raised judged surprise from $1.8$ to $\sim
3.0$ --- the flagship positive of the program --- while constant
pressure (a continuity connective) was worse than nothing
\citep{ono2026interrupting}. At the \emph{weight} level, consolidating
a model's own verified findings moved the mean toward the known good
and never beyond it, paying for mass with tails \citep{ono2026mass};
in verified search, operator packages composed additively and stalled
at a construction-family plateau (companion paper, in moderation at
the time of writing). Between the context and the token sits the object
none of these touched: the residual stream $h_\ell(t)$ --- the thought
in progress. Recent work proves that steered activations reach states
that no discrete prompt reaches~\citep{mishra2026nonsurjective}, but
leaves their \emph{value} untested; activation steering itself is an
industrial control tool --- removing behaviors, enforcing
styles~\citep{turner2023actadd,panickssery2023caa,li2023iti,zou2023repe,arditi2024refusal}
--- that has not been pointed at creation under a deception-resistant
measure.

This paper does that. Its contributions:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{An instrument}: a rank-1/2 operator algebra on the
  residual stream (\S\ref{sec:instrument}) with model-derived
  directions, geometric guards, and a dimensionless dose unit that
  transfers across models; dual MLX/PyTorch backends, bit-identical on
  the algebra.
\item \textbf{The temporal law} (\S\ref{sec:temporal}): continuous
  injection has no fertile band --- more dose, less judged surprise ---
  while the same vectors, pulsed, are fertile. The decisive variable is
  \emph{when}, not \emph{what} or \emph{how much}.
\item \textbf{Parity} (\S\ref{sec:parity}): guards trade repair for
  dose, and the guarded pulse at dose 2.0 statistically ties text
  interruption on judged surprise with identical connection ---
  replicating in-family at 14B with an exact tie
  (\S\ref{sec:generality}).
\item \textbf{A mechanism} (\S\ref{sec:mechanism}): habituation leaves
  a silent schematic orbit, measurable as matched token--state
  periodicity; interruption and the pulse both flatten it.
\item \textbf{A dissociation} (\S\ref{sec:verified}): on verified code
  search the same operators buy zero tail depth --- the state channel
  moves judged experience, not discovered value.
\end{enumerate}

Every confirmatory analysis below was pre-registered (grid, primary
contrast, guards, verdict tiers) and written as code before the data
existed; exploratory screens are labeled as such. All deltas are paired
per premise over the ten held-out premises of
\citet{ono2026interrupting}'s confirmatory battery, with exact sign-flip
tests ($2^{10}$) and bootstrap 95\% CIs.

\section{Instrument and protocol}
\label{sec:instrument}

\paragraph{Operators.} All operators are rank-1/2 updates from stored
unit vectors, $O(d)$ per position; no $d\times d$ matrix is ever
materialized. With $\hat u,\hat w$ orthonormal and
$c_u=\langle h,\hat u\rangle$: \emph{translation} $h + \alpha v$;
\emph{rotor} $h + \sin\theta\,(c_u \hat w - c_w \hat u) +
(\cos\theta-1)(c_u \hat u + c_w \hat w)$, the closed (Rodrigues) form of
$\exp\!\big(\theta(\hat w\hat u^{\top}-\hat u\hat w^{\top})\big)$,
verified against the matrix exponential to $10^{-10}$;
\emph{projection/dilation} of a subspace; \emph{repulsion}
$h + \beta\,(h-\mu)/\lVert h-\mu\rVert$ from an exponential moving
average $\mu$ of the trajectory. Two composable \emph{guards}: an
anchor that restores the coordinate along the premise direction after
the operator, and a norm shell that rescales $\lVert h\rVert$ to its
pre-operator value.

\paragraph{Directions and dose.} Concept directions are the model's
own: the unit mean hidden state of a concept text at the target layer
(position 0 excluded), differenced against the premise state. Doses are
expressed in units of the premise-state norm at that layer, making
$\alpha$ dimensionless and, as \S\ref{sec:generality} shows,
transferable across models without retuning.

\paragraph{Carrier and measurement.} Unless noted, generation runs on a
base model (Qwen3-8B) with the habituation of
\citet{ono2026interrupting} (a count-weighted log-penalty over the last
512 tokens) --- the regime in which text interruption was established.
Judging follows the generated-only protocol: 96-token windows that
never contain injected text, scored on surprise, connection and
coherence by a $k{=}5$ judge panel whose test--retest behavior was
measured in the original study. A cheap coherence proxy (mean NLL of
the stream under the same model, untouched) and distinct-4 accompany
every cell. Verified experiments (\S\ref{sec:verified}) reuse the exact
bin-packing evaluator and classical baselines of \citet{ono2026mass}.

\section{The temporal law}
\label{sec:temporal}

\paragraph{Continuous injection has no fertile band.} An exploratory
depth$\times$dose map (57 cells) located the semantic lever in the
middle of the network: early-layer injection collapses generation into
memorized register basins (a math-homework attractor), late-layer
injection acts as token bias --- at high dose the concept's own token
fragment becomes a literal loop --- and only mid-network injection
transports content. The pre-registered confirmatory then swept the
candidate band (layers 14/18/22, $\alpha\in[0.1,1.5]$, continuous
schedule, 140 cells) with judges. The primary failed in both halves:
band-core surprise minus the habituated baseline $-0.478$
\ci{-1.478}{+0.522}, one-sided $p=0.81$, and the coherence guard
failed ($-1.08$). The dose--response is monotone the wrong way:
surprise $2.38 \to 0.85$ and coherence $4.83 \to 1.38$ as $\alpha$
rises to 1.5 (Fig.~\ref{fig:templaw}). More push makes text that a
judge finds \emph{less} surprising: degeneration is boring. Notably,
the prompt-matched control --- the same concept injected as text into
this plain carrier --- also failed to beat the baseline, reproducing
the original finding that novelty injection needs habituation as its
carrier \citep{ono2026interrupting}.

\paragraph{The same vectors, pulsed, are fertile.} The pre-registered
transposition of the interruption's temporal structure --- bursts of 32
tokens every 300, directions rotating over the same four new-subject
texts used by text interruption, on the habituated carrier --- raises
judged surprise by $+0.90$ \ci{+0.45}{+1.33} over habituation
($p=0.0059$, coherence guard passed). Same directions, same layer, same
per-token dose as a continuous regime that failed at $p=0.81$: the
decisive variable is the temporal regime. Text interruption replicates
in the same battery ($+1.76$, $p=0.0029$), anchoring the instrument.

\section{Guards license dose; parity with the written word}
\label{sec:parity}

Pulsing at higher dose without protection costs coherence ($-1.01$
against text interruption, $p=0.049$). The pre-registered guard test
shows the guards do \emph{not} repair this: at equal dose, anchor +
norm-shell change coherence by $+0.02$ ($p=0.50$) while visibly
weakening the effective push (clean NLL $2.53 \to 1.83$). What they do
is license dose: under guards the ladder $\alpha = 1.0 \to 1.5 \to 2.0$
climbs monotonically in judged surprise ($2.10 \to 3.23 \to 3.40$) at
roughly constant coherence (Fig.~\ref{fig:ladder}).

At the top of that ladder sits the pre-registered comparison against
the program's strongest prompt-side intervention. The verdict tier that
fired is the middle one: \textbf{parity}. Guarded pulse at dose 2.0
versus text interruption: surprise $3.40$ vs $3.64$ ($\Delta = -0.24$
\ci{-0.94}{+0.47}, two-sided $p=0.53$, $|\Delta|<0.5$), connection
$2.38$ vs $2.39$, coherence within the pre-registered guard. A subject
can be placed into the model's stream as pure activation --- invisible
in the text, spending no tokens, continuously dosable --- and a judge
panel cannot tell the difference from writing it down. We emphasize
what parity is not: the strong hypothesis, that state injection reaches
value no prompt reaches, remains open ($\S$\ref{sec:discussion}).

Two declared secondaries returned honest negatives. A first-version
rotor (rotating the premise--subject plane, $\theta=0.8$) underperforms
text interruption ($-1.53$, $p=0.014$; a later cheap screen located its
useful range at $\theta \in [1.2, 1.6]$ --- an untuned operator, not a
dead one). And the program's own registered prediction failed: pulsed
state repulsion \emph{without} an anchor did not degenerate (its
coherence was the highest of the state arms), refusing the fourth
appearance of the anchor law; a stress screen then showed why ---
repulsion from a \emph{moving} mean is self-limiting, because the
reference chases the state. The anchor law, as established in weights
and behavior, is about fixed references.

\section{Generality: the parity travels in-family}
\label{sec:generality}

The pre-registered generality battery reran the decisive trio ---
habituated baseline, guarded pulse at dose 2.0, text interruption ---
on three untouched models, with the layer at 50\% of each network's
depth and the dose carried over unchanged in premise-norm units.

\textbf{Qwen3-14B (same family, larger): full replication.} Pulse over
habituation $+1.083$ \ci{+0.567}{+1.617}, $p=0.0020$, coherence guard
clean; and parity with text interruption lands as an exact tie:
surprise $3.48$ vs $3.48$ ($p=0.99$). The dimensionless dose and
proportional-depth layer transferred without retuning.

\textbf{Qwen3.8-27B (current generation, aligned): the carrier breaks,
not the phenomenon.} Under our base-model protocol --- which bans EOS
and forces 1500 tokens --- this chat-trained model opens with fluent
prose and collapses into token salad past its natural turn end
(judge notes: ``degenerate token soup''). A nominally significant
pulse effect inside that salad ($p=0.041$) does not count as support,
and we do not count it. Aligned carriers need their own protocol
(chat-formatted seeding, free EOS, natural lengths); this is future
work, not a footnote.

\textbf{OLMo-2-13B (different family): a real null with a carrier
caveat.} Pulse minus habituation $-0.09$ ($p=0.78$). All three arms,
however, sit in an encyclopedic register basin (judged coherence
$0.4$--$0.6$ everywhere; heavy judge-refusal rate), the OLMo analogue
of the register basins of \S\ref{sec:temporal}. Whether the null
reflects family-dependence of the phenomenon or miscalibration of
layer and dose for this family cannot be separated here; per-model
calibration is registered future work. The generality claim we make is
therefore exactly this: \emph{the parity travels across scale within a
family; family-generality is open}.

\section{The mechanism: silent orbits, flattened spectra}
\label{sec:mechanism}

Why does the pulse work where pressure fails? A measurement study
replayed finished cells through the model, capturing the mid-layer
trajectory, and compared the state autocorrelation
$A_{\mathrm{state}}(\tau)$ (unit-normed, mean-centered states) against
the token autocorrelation (the ground truth of literal loops), reading
the strongest detrended local peak of each ($\tau \ge 5$; literal
period-2 loops sit below the reader's floor and are noted as a blind
spot).

The prominence ladder is clean (Fig.~\ref{fig:spectra}). A literal
phrase loop shows a state period matching the phrase length ($P=7$,
prominence $0.44$). Register collapses --- the quiz mode, the
math-exercise mode --- show matched token--state periodicity at their
block rhythms ($P=123$ and $84$). And the habituated carrier, which
repeats no tokens, still carries a \emph{silent schematic orbit}:
matched token--state periodicity at $P\approx 30$ (prominence
$0.18/0.15$) whose text reads as sentence-level cycling through the
same cast --- the ``return to the theme, not the sentence'' of
\citet{ono2026interrupting}, now visible as a rhythm in the state.
Interruption, the pulse, and state repulsion all flatten this internal
spectrum (prominence $\le 0.014$). The interventions that judges
reward are, mechanically, spectral flatteners of the thought
trajectory.

\section{The verified dissociation}
\label{sec:verified}

Do these operators buy anything a verifier can price? The
pre-registered battery ran three arms --- untouched carrier, pulses
toward narrative subjects (the cross-register wildcard), pulses toward
the domain's own thinking prompts --- inside verified bin-packing
notebook generation (16 distribution variants including the far family
of \citet{ono2026mass}, 6 notebooks each, 288 notebooks), scoring with
the exact evaluator against best-fit and first-fit.

The answer is a clean null with a striking signature. Per-variant best
distance to the classics: wildcard minus control $+0.00014$
\ci{+0.00000}{+0.00036}, one-sided $p=1.0$; transfer and far-family
contrasts null; and the single best gap across \emph{all three arms} is
identical to five decimals ($-0.00334$, three times). At this scale the
reachable tail depth behaves as a constant of the model and carrier,
untouched by state stimulation. (An earlier 120-notebook screen had
produced a lone wildcard find; the confirmatory dissolved it into
$n{=}3$ sampling noise --- all arms find occasionally at $n{=}6$ ---
which is the two-gear system doing its job.) A first-version
route-blocking operator --- projecting out the top principal directions
of a previous solution's trajectory --- behaved as a mild handicap:
production down, no depth gained, and a calibration curve showing that
the top trajectory components carry the code-writing machinery itself
(blocking $\ge 4$ directions is lobotomy).

Together with \S\ref{sec:parity}, this is the paper's deepest result:
\emph{the state channel moves judged experience, not discovered value}.
The operators that tie the written word in a judge's eyes buy zero
verified tail --- the oldest lesson of the program (surface novelty is
not value), returned at the deepest substrate, and consistent with the
attractor results of \citet{ono2026mass}.

\section{Boundaries}
\label{sec:boundaries}

For designers of state interventions, the mapped edges: (i) \emph{depth
matters more than dose} --- early layers destabilize into register
basins, late layers are token bias (at high dose the injected concept's
token fragment loops literally: the late network is a mouth, not a
mind); (ii) \emph{guards are throttles, not medicine}; (iii)
\emph{moving-reference repulsion is self-limiting} --- collapse
requires a fixed reference, which reframes the anchor law; (iv)
\emph{aligned models are a different carrier} --- forced continuation
past the turn end manufactures degeneration that no operator caused;
(v) judged-surprise instruments punish degeneration (they cannot be
gamed by noise), but they refuse some degenerate text outright, which
costs coverage and must be reported.

\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Activation steering.} Additive steering vectors
\citep{turner2023actadd,panickssery2023caa}, truthfulness intervention
\citep{li2023iti}, representation engineering \citep{zou2023repe}, SAE
features \citep{cunningham2023sae}, function vectors
\citep{todd2023function} and refusal-direction ablation
\citep{arditi2024refusal} steer style, safety and factual behavior;
industrial agents already use such edits routinely for control
\citep{chen2026automated}. STARS \citep{zhu2026stars} is closest in
spirit --- multiple orthogonal steering directions for diverse
generation --- but evaluates without a prompt-matched rival or a
deception-resistant judge protocol, and uses translations only.
\citet{mishra2026nonsurjective} prove steered states are unreachable by
prompts but do not test their value; our parity-not-superiority result
is the first value measurement in that opened space, and it constrains
easy versions of the fertility hypothesis. Weight-space analogues ---
task arithmetic \citep{ilharco2022task}, creativity weight-steering
\citep{schapiro2026creativityneuro} --- edit the persistent model where
we perturb the transient thought.

\paragraph{Mechanistic intervention.} Causal tracing and model editing
record and modify internal routes to \emph{explain} behavior
\citep{meng2022rome}; our route-blocking screen inverts the direction
--- recording a route to \emph{forbid} it --- and reports the first
calibration of how much of a trajectory can be blocked before the
faculty, not the idea, is removed.

\paragraph{Decoding-time control.} Degeneration under maximization
\citep{holtzman2019degeneration} anticipates our salad regimes;
contrastive decoding \citep{li2022contrastive} and proxy tuning
\citep{liu2024proxy} intervene through logits of a second model, a
surface channel our late-layer results effectively reproduce
(late-layer injection $\approx$ token bias); the mid-network channel
studied here is precisely what logit-level methods cannot reach.

\paragraph{The program.} Protocol, habituation and interruption from
\citet{ono2026interrupting}; consolidation, tails and the attractor
from \citet{ono2026mass}; the operator-package factorial from the
companion verified-search paper. The AAR study
\citep{chen2026automated} independently reproduces the program's
telemetry (method monocultures, flat idea diversity under climbing
scores, capability anchors) at industrial scale in an
alignment-mitigation setting --- a mass regime, complementary to the
frontier questions here.

\section{Discussion and limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What parity means.} A new channel into the model's ongoing
thought exists, is dosable and reversible, spends no context, is
invisible in the text, and matches the best prompt-side intervention
under a judge panel that cannot see either intervention. What it does
not mean: superiority. The strong fertility hypothesis --- that
prompt-unreachable states yield value prompts cannot --- remains open;
our continuous-injection null and the state$\approx$prompt tie in the
easy setting are its first constraining data points.

\paragraph{Experience versus value.} The dissociation is the finding we
expect to age best: state operators changed what a judge experiences
and left untouched what a verifier prices. Any proposal that
inference-time steering will unlock discovery must now say why its
mechanism differs from the ones measured here.

\paragraph{Limitations.} The ten premises are reused across this
paper's batteries (each analysis pre-registered before its data, but
adaptation risk accumulates and is declared); the judge panel is a
single instrument family (its test--retest behavior was measured in
\citealp{ono2026interrupting}); generality is established across scale,
not family; doses and layers were calibrated on the origin model and
transferred by convention (50\% depth, premise-norm units) rather than
per-model tuning; and the verified domain is one task family. The
temporal law's mechanism (why 32-token pulses integrate where constant
pressure degrades) is characterized spectrally but not causally.

\paragraph{Future work.} A protocol for aligned carriers; per-model
calibration for cross-family tests; the strong fertility test on a
carrier where the pulse and the word are both at ceiling; and
substrate-level questions --- consolidating the search's schematic
memory rather than its winners, and cross-model state transfer ---
that the present results motivate but do not touch.
