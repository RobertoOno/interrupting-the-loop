\section{Conclusion}

Where does novelty come from when a language model writes with no task? Not
from the sampler: an anti-probable decoder buys surface novelty that never
rises to the level of ideas. Not from the prompt: improbable inputs are noise.
It comes from the loop, and from a simple part of it. A base model feeding on
its own output freezes; keep it from eating its literal past and interrupt it,
every few hundred tokens, with a sentence that leads away, over everything it
remembers, and it comes alive: it develops, is broken, and finds its own way
back, and that is what a reader (human, or one of two model families) calls
surprise with connection. Inside the network the working interruption is a
surface event over an intact deep state; the deep re-encounter is forgetting,
and it is rewarded less. Creativity, in this system, is in interrupting the
loop.
