\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Ten seeds, all English narrative premises. Three generator families for the
loop's core ladder, one (Qwen3-30B-A3B) for the content, timing and frequency
battery. LLM judges: Opus~5, calibrated, agreeing with a second family
(Kimi K2.6, $\rho=0.71$--$0.85$) and with the consensus of independent human
readers ($\rho=0.58$ on surprise, $0.52$ on coherence, three raters so far),
whose agreement with each other is moderate ($\alpha=0.30$--$0.38$). The judged sample of each cell is six
windows per review kind, evenly spread; salience-timed arms are judged on
selected moments and on a uniform grid, and both are reported, because the
first is a selection. The residual-stream analysis is correlational, uses
mean-pooled 64-token windows at 13 layers, and does not touch attention or
causal interventions. The sampler's null inside the loop is a null at the
instrument's resolution ($\approx$1 point). Whether the rhythm ($\approx$300
tokens) and the ``lead away, then let it come back on its own'' rule hold for
other genres and for tasks with a verifier is the next question, and the
fusion the program points to: an interrupted loop over a \emph{problem}, with a
verifier in place of the judge.
