\paragraph{Acknowledgements and tooling.} The experiments, analyses and text of this
paper were produced by the author working with Claude (Anthropic) as a
programming and writing assistant inside the Claude Code environment, and the
corrections of version~2 were prepared the same way; every design decision,
result and claim was reviewed by the author. The code and the dated
laboratory notebook are public in the repository, and the run data and LLM
judgments are in the evidence deposit
(\href{https://doi.org/\evidencedoi}{doi:\evidencedoi}).
Judges were run on Amazon Bedrock and OpenRouter; generators ran locally on
Apple silicon via MLX.

\paragraph{Changes in version 2.} This version corrects version~1 (20 August
2026); no conclusion changes. Version~1 is the preprint; what the text calls
the first version of this study is the earlier, pre-review draft that used
the event-window protocol (\texttt{docs/PAPER\_DREAM.md} in the repository).
(i)~Status: the second round of human ratings, described in version~1 as in
progress, was prepared but not run. (ii)~Human validation: the human readers rated only surprise and coherence, on windows
of the first protocol, which could contain the injected sentence; the
introduction and the conclusion now state that scope, and the agreement with
the second judge family is given for both of its samples. (iii)~Self-copy:
the abstract ran together two measures, the share of post-interruption
windows that are replay (65--80\% at periods 150--300) and the share whose
source lies beyond the judge's horizon (62\% at period 300); they are now
separate. (iv)~Numbers: the main-text numbers of Section~\ref{sec:loop} and
of the appendix text now match the appendix tables. In version~1 some had been computed before the last re-judged
windows were added; most differences are below 0.1 points and none exceeds
0.3. With the final values the fresh-only effect on connection is +0.7 to
+0.9 (version~1: +0.8), the fresh-only effect on surprise is only slightly
smaller than the all-windows one (version~1: about one point smaller), and
the fresh-only connection gain on the other generators is now reported, three
counts of premises moving the same way are given as the final values show them
(for example eight of ten, not every premise, for reset against preserved context on
surprise), and differences on coherence once said to have intervals that include
zero are described as not significant at $n=10$. For rater privacy, the individual
raters' means and agreements are now given as ranges and the payment without
amounts. Three statements were corrected: the salience-timed stitch was called the
arm the judge rates lowest, which held only under the first protocol;
``two to three points'' of surprise on all windows holds against bare
generation, not against habituation; and the abstract said that a paragraph
break adds nothing detectable ``on fresh text'', for which no contrast is
reported (the all-windows contrast is).
(v)~Availability: version~1 said that the per-run data, judgments and rater
files were released with the repository; they were not. The run data and the
LLM judgments are now in the evidence deposit, and individual human ratings
are not released, to protect rater anonymity. A duplicated table caption and
a table reference that pointed to the wrong table were fixed.
